The idea

Your face stops being the bottleneck. Content keeps shipping while you're on holiday, in a meeting, or just not camera-ready.

Personal brands don't break on ideas. They break on shoot days: lighting, tripod, re-takes, "not today". The twin takes all of that off you. You approve the script on your phone and the video goes out.

FAQ

What people ask first.

Can people tell it isn't me?

On vertical video under 60 seconds, effectively no. We separately clean up eye contact, pauses and hand gestures — that is where the eye catches a fake. Before launch you get a test take and you either say "that's me" or we rebuild it; revisions aren't capped at that stage.

How much footage do you need from me?

Four minutes of video and fifteen minutes of speech. That fits into 40 minutes including the lighting setup. Shoot at our studio, or at yours — in that case we ship lights, a lav mic and a framing guide.

Who owns the avatar and the voice?

You do. We sign a likeness and voice release plus a contract that leaves the twin and every deliverable with you. On Premiere we hand over the source files and models at the end. If you leave, the twin is deleted from our accounts on your request.

What if I need to say something urgently?

Standard turnaround is 48 hours from approved copy. Scene and Premiere include urgent takes: send the text, get the finished video back in 24 hours — overnight on Premiere.

How does paying for all the AI tools work?

Every subscription — HeyGen, ElevenLabs, background generation — sits on our accounts and is inside the plan. You get one invoice a month, on contract, with proper paperwork. No card details in five dashboards and no credit running out mid-month.

What if the content doesn't land?

We treat month one as a test: we watch three-second retention and completion rate, then swap hooks and rubrics. If you stop after the first month the twin stays with you, with nothing more to pay for it.
